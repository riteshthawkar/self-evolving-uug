
\section{Related Work}

Recent progress on unified multimodal models (UMMs) has shifted the field from \emph{capability co-existence} (one model that can both understand and generate) toward \emph{capability co-evolution} (one capability explicitly improving the other during post-training). The core question is no longer whether a model can perform both tasks, but whether understanding and generation become mutually reinforcing under self-supervised or weakly supervised updates.

\paragraph{Role-based self-evolution and internal evaluators.}
A prominent direction decomposes a single UMM into interacting roles and trains them in self-play. UniCorn instantiates a Proposer--Solver--Judge triad and performs cognitive pattern reconstruction from self-generated interactions, targeting generation gains without sacrificing understanding \citep{han2026unicorn}. Endogenous Reprompting (SEER) introduces an endogenous evaluator--reprompter loop, where RLVR first activates latent evaluation ability and RLMT optimizes generation-time reasoning (``thinking policy'') from model-derived rewards \citep{tang2026endogenous}. UniGame frames post-training as self-adversarial alignment by adding a lightweight perturber at the shared interface, making generation actively expose fragile understanding regions \citep{su2025unigame}. SRUM similarly uses understanding as an internal evaluator for generation, but emphasizes global-local reward decomposition for multi-scale corrective feedback \citep{jin2025srum}. EvoLMM is conceptually close in proposer--solver structure, but differs by explicitly using continuous self-consistency rewards over unlabeled images \citep{thawakar2025evolmm}.

\paragraph{Generation--understanding coupling via reconstruction and internal-gap exploitation.}
A second line of work improves unification through reconstruction-style objectives and internal-gap mining. UAE (``Can Understanding and Generation Truly Benefit Together -- or Just Coexist?'') formalizes UMM training through an auto-encoder lens (I2T as encoder, T2I as decoder), optimized with staged Unified-GRPO around reconstruction fidelity \citep{yan2025understandinggeneration}. RecA performs resource-efficient reconstruction alignment by conditioning generation on internal understanding embeddings, improving fidelity with modest post-training cost \citep{xie2025reconstruction}. Turning Internal Gap into Self-Improvement constructs post-training data by scoring generation with stronger understanding modules, then applies curriculum-style SFT/DPO refinement \citep{han2025internalgap}. UniMRG studies the inverse transfer direction directly, i.e., generation-side auxiliary representations (pixel/depth/segmentation) to strengthen understanding \citep{su2026unimrg}.

\paragraph{RL-centric bidirectional optimization.}
Several methods cast unification as explicit reinforcement optimization over both tasks. UniRL performs iterative self-training with generated images and uses SFT+GRPO to improve understanding and generation jointly without relying on external image corpora during post-training \citep{mao2025unirl}. CoRL proposes a unified RL stage followed by task-refinement RL stages, reporting strong gains from joint policy optimization \citep{jiang2025coreinforcement}. Reinforcing Multimodal Understanding and Generation with Dual Self-rewards (SUDER line) uses dual likelihood rewards by reversing task directions, avoiding external reward models \citep{hong2025dualselfrewards}. Skywork UniPic 2.0 shows a strong engineering variant with online RL and progressive dual-task reinforcement for unified generation/editing+understanding systems \citep{wei2025skyworkunipic2}. VARGPT-v1.1 follows an iterative instruction tuning + RL recipe for visual autoregressive unified training \citep{zhuang2025vargpt}. SILMM, although primarily compositional T2I-focused, is an important self-improvement baseline using iterative self-feedback and DPO/continuous-DPO style optimization \citep{qu2024silmm}. ILLUME contributes a self-enhancing multimodal alignment scheme where the model self-assesses consistency between text and self-generated images \citep{wang2024illume}.

\paragraph{Reward-model-centric unification components.}
UnifiedReward provides a shared reward model spanning understanding and generation assessment and is often used as an enabler for downstream preference optimization pipelines rather than a fully autonomous self-evolving loop by itself \citep{wang2025unifiedreward}. This class of methods can be effective but introduces dependence on reward-model data construction and reward-model transferability assumptions.

\paragraph{Benchmarks diagnosing whether unification is real.}
A parallel body of work argues that many UMMs still exhibit ``surface-level'' rather than ``deep'' unification. UniSandbox provides controlled analysis to test whether understanding truly informs generation and identifies persistent reasoning-transfer gaps \citep{niu2025unisandbox}. GapEval quantifies bidirectional coherence explicitly and reports that capability emergence and cross-modal knowledge synchronization often remain misaligned across current UMM families \citep{wang2026gapeval}. MME-Unify further broadens evaluation coverage for unified understanding+generation settings \citep{xie2025mmeunify}. These studies motivate training frameworks that optimize bidirectional coupling directly, rather than improving each task in isolation.

\paragraph{Positioning of our framework.}
Our framework is closest in spirit to role-based and RL-based self-evolving UMMs, but differs in several design choices that are central to our claims. First, unlike one-direction transfer methods (predominantly U$\rightarrow$G or G$\rightarrow$U), our training loop performs explicit alternating bidirectional updates in a closed cycle. Second, compared to methods centered on a single scalar supervision source (e.g., pure reconstruction similarity or single-form self-likelihood), our objective combines multiple self-derived signals, enabling denser optimization in open-ended settings. Third, compared to approaches that require dedicated external reward models, our core loop is self-contained and keeps evaluator extensions optional. Fourth, compared to post-training schemes tightly coupled to a specific architecture family, our implementation is intentionally model-agnostic at the framework level (role decomposition + cyclic optimization + self-generated supervision), which improves portability across unified backbones.

\paragraph{Practical comparison axes used in this paper.}
To make comparisons reproducible and fair, we evaluate prior work and our method along five axes: (1) \emph{self-supervision purity} (degree of reliance on external labels/reward models), (2) \emph{bidirectionality} (U$\leftrightarrow$G both optimized vs one-way transfer), (3) \emph{reward richness} (single vs multi-component self-derived objectives), (4) \emph{stability controls} (collapse prevention, curriculum, and filtering mechanisms), and (5) \emph{architecture dependence}. We use these axes to explain not only final performance, but also why some frameworks improve one side while leaving the understanding--generation gap largely unresolved.
