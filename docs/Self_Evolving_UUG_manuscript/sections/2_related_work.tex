
\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\noindent\textbf{Unified visual understanding and image generation models.}
Unified models that jointly perform visual understanding and image generation have advanced rapidly. Representative designs include early fusion (Chameleon~\citep{team2024chameleon}), decoupled encoders (Janus/Janus-Pro~\citep{wu2025janus, chen2025januspro}), and token-level unification (Show-o, VILA-U~\citep{xie2024showo, wu2024vilau}). Modern unified backbones span diffusion (BLIP3o), flow matching (BAGEL), and autoregressive generation (VARGPT-v1.1) \citep{chen2025blip3o, deng2025bagel, zhuang2025vargpt}. However, most improvements still rely on supervised post-training with curated data, motivating fully self-supervised post-training for both tasks.

\noindent\textbf{Role-based self-play and self-improvement.}
Role-based self-play decomposes a unified model into interacting components that generate supervision internally. UniCorn~\citep{han2026unicorn} uses Proposer, Solver, and Judge roles to bridge the gap between visual understanding and image generation. However, it lacks token-level uncertainty signals and multi-scale checks for generation. UniGame~\citep{su2025unigame} perturbs a shared token interface to expose fragile representations, while SEER~\citep{tang2026endogenous} activates an internal evaluator via a proxy RL stage. EvoLMM~\citep{thawakar2025evolmm} is closest in spirit (continuous self-consistency rewards) but improves understanding only and remains vulnerable when sample-level consistency becomes uninformative. Overall, prior self-play methods often miss (i) a robust difficulty signal under reward degeneracy and (ii) an internal evaluator that separates local attribute errors from global scene coherence.

\noindent\textbf{Reconstruction and internal gap methods.}
Another line couples visual understanding and image generation through reconstruction or by exploiting the common visual understanding$>$image generation asymmetry. UAE~\citep{yan2025understandinggeneration} uses a staged reconstruction pipeline but depends on encoder--decoder coupling and a single reconstruction signal. RecA~\citep{xie2025reconstruction} conditions generation on internal embeddings, trading portability for efficiency. Internal Gap~\citep{han2025internalgap} uses the visual understanding module to score self-generated images and construct SFT/DPO data, but reduces co-improvement to a single scalar score without multi-scale assessment or collapse detection.

\noindent\textbf{Reinforcement-based optimization.}
RL-based post-training treats both visual understanding and image generation as policy optimization. UniRL~\citep{mao2025unirl} and CoRL~\citep{jiang2025coreinforcement} show that RL can improve unified models. However, these approaches rely on ground-truth rewards or task-specific reward engineering. SUDER~\citep{hong2025dualselfrewards} leverages visual understanding--image generation duality to avoid external reward models. SRUM~\citep{jin2025srum} decomposes spatial rewards, but still lacks a portable token-level signal to detect reward degeneracy. Other approaches either focus on generation-only self-improvement (SILMM~\citep{qu2024silmm}) or bootstrap evaluators via external LLMs (ILLUME~\citep{wang2024illume}), departing from the fully self-supervised setting.

\noindent\textbf{Our approach.}
Table~\ref{tab:qualitative_comparison} summarizes prior work by supervision, tasks improved, architecture dependence, and generalizability. Our framework uses only unlabeled images and three lightweight LoRA roles on a frozen backbone, deriving training signals from self-consistency, token-level entropy (STE), and multi-scale internal evaluation for image generation (QA fidelity + cycle-consistent captioning).

Although several methods claim generality, none validates an unchanged, self-supervised recipe across all three major generation paradigms. UniCorn~\citep{han2026unicorn} spans flow matching and autoregressive backbones but omits diffusion; UniRL~\citep{mao2025unirl} requires ground-truth rewards; Internal Gap~\citep{han2025internalgap} is self-supervised but tested only within the autoregressive family. In practice, paradigm transfer is fragile: diffusion, flow matching, and autoregressive models induce different optimization landscapes, so rewards can silently degenerate without multi-scale checks and token-level uncertainty. We provide empirical evidence of consistent gains across diffusion (BLIP3o), flow matching (BAGEL), and autoregressive (VARGPT-v1.1) backbones with no per-backbone adaptation.
