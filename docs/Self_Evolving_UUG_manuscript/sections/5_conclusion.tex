\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a self-evolving training framework that enables unified understanding and generation models to improve both capabilities autonomously using only unlabeled images. The framework decomposes a frozen backbone into three co-evolving roles, a proposer, solver, and generator, and derives all training signals from self-consistency and token-level entropy without human annotations, curated instruction data, or external reward models. Solver Token Entropy addresses the degenerate-signal limitation of self-consistency by providing a continuous difficulty measure from a single greedy forward pass, enabling the proposer to maintain effective curriculum pressure throughout training. The solver's dual role as both learner and internal evaluator couples understanding improvement directly to generation quality through multi-scale assessment combining QA fidelity scoring and cycle-consistent captioning.

We validated the framework on three architecturally distinct unified models spanning diffusion-based, flow-matching, and autoregressive generation paradigms, applying the same algorithm with identical hyperparameters across all three. The results demonstrate that unsupervised self-evolution can serve as a scalable and general-purpose post-training strategy for unified models.

\noindent\textbf{Limitations and future work.} The current framework uses the understanding pathway as the sole evaluator for generation quality, meaning that generation assessment is bounded by the solver's perceptual capabilities. Incorporating the generation pathway as a complementary evaluator for understanding, provided that the generation-to-understanding transfer can be experimentally confirmed, could enable fully symmetric co-evolution. Additionally, while our results demonstrate that the self-evolving loop operates effectively from a cold start, investigating longer training horizons and the conditions under which self-improvement saturates remains an important direction. Finally, extending the framework to additional modalities beyond image understanding and generation, such as video or audio, would test the broader applicability of role-based self-supervised optimization.
