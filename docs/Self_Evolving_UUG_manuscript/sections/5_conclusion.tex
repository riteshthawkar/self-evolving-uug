\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a self-evolving training framework that enables unified understanding and generation models to improve both capabilities using only unlabeled images, without human annotations, curated data, or external reward models. The framework decomposes a frozen backbone into three co-evolving roles via LoRA adapters: a Proposer, Solver, and Generator. Solver Token Entropy addresses the degenerate-signal limitation of self-consistency by providing a continuous difficulty measure from a single greedy pass, enabling effective curriculum learning. The Solver's dual role as both learner and internal evaluator couples understanding quality directly to generation through multi-scale assessment combining QA fidelity scoring and cycle-consistent captioning. Validation on three architecturally distinct unified models spanning diffusion-based, flow-matching, and autoregressive paradigms demonstrates that self-evolving post-training is a scalable and general-purpose strategy for unified models.

\noindent\textbf{Limitations.} The framework uses understanding as the sole evaluator for generation, bounding assessment by the Solver's perceptual capability. Investigating longer training horizons and conditions under which self-improvement saturates, as well as extending to additional modalities such as video, remain important future directions.
