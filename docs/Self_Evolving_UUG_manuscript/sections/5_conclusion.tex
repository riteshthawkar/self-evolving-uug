\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a self-evolving framework for unified understanding and generation models that uses only unlabeled images and no external supervision. The method decomposes a frozen backbone into three LoRA-based roles (Proposer, Solver, Generator), uses STE for continuous difficulty-aware understanding feedback, and uses Solver-driven QA fidelity with cycle-consistent captioning for generation training. Across BLIP3o, BAGEL, and VARGPT$_{1.1}$, this single framework improves understanding metrics by +2.0 to +3.5 points over base checkpoints and improves GenEval by +0.03 on every backbone. These results support the central objective of this work: model-agnostic, unsupervised self-evolution for jointly improving understanding and generation.

\noindent\textbf{Limitations.} The framework uses understanding as the sole evaluator for generation, bounding assessment by the Solver's perceptual capability. Investigating longer training horizons and conditions under which self-improvement saturates, as well as extending to additional modalities such as video, remain important future directions.
